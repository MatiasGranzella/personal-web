How I work.

  1. 01First call

    Which questions or tasks you want to automate, with what data and on which channel: your website, WhatsApp or an internal tool.

  2. 02Prototype with your data

    A first version tested on real cases from your business, to see quickly whether it answers well.

  3. 03Evaluation before launch

    We build a set of questions with expected answers and measure how many it gets right. If it doesn’t reach the agreed bar, it doesn’t ship.

  4. 04Production and monitoring

    I connect it to your systems and set up metrics and traces to see what people ask, what the bot answers and where it fails.

FAQ.

Can it work on WhatsApp?

Yes. The same bot can serve customers on WhatsApp, on your website or inside an internal tool; we pick the channel on the first call.

What happens when the bot doesn’t know the answer?

I build it to say so and hand the conversation to a person. Making up answers is exactly what the evaluation measures and fixes.

What about my customers’ data?

I use the model providers’ APIs, which don’t train on that data. We decide together what the bot can and can’t see.

Do you work with clients outside Argentina?

Yes, remotely and in English. I’m based in Buenos Aires (GMT-3), which overlaps well with US and European working hours.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the channels, integrations and number of cases it has to handle. After the first call I send you a quote.
